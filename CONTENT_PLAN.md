# Quiick Medicine daily social content plan

Posts publish AUTOMATICALLY to Facebook and Instagram (Metricool brand 7849582). No human reviews them,
so every post must be safe to go live as-is.

## Hard rules (never break)
- General pharmacy and wellness education only.
- NO prescription drug names, NO GLP-1 / semaglutide / Ozempic / weight-loss drugs, NO hormone therapy,
  NO compounded-medication promotion, NO prices, discounts, or "buy/shop now" pushes for medications.
- NO health claims (cures, treats, prevents), no before/after, no body-image content.
- No dosing advice beyond "follow your label / ask your pharmacist".
- Always end captions with: "For general awareness only. Follow your healthcare professional's advice."
- Canadian spelling. Friendly, plain language.

## Topic ideas (rotate; check posts/ to avoid repeats within 30 days)
- Questions to ask your pharmacist
- How to read a medication label
- Myth vs fact (antibiotics and colds, expired meds, etc.)
- Where to store medications
- Safe disposal of old medications (take-back at the pharmacy)
- Prescription transfers: how easy it is to switch pharmacies
- Free Ontario-wide delivery / local North York pickup
- Meet the pharmacy / what a compounding pharmacy is (general, no product claims)
- Flu and cold season habits; handwashing
- Travel health checklist
- Keeping an up-to-date medication list
- Health awareness days and months (Canadian calendar: e.g. Diabetes Awareness Month in November,
  World Diabetes Day Nov 14). Awareness only, no product pushes.
- Seasonal: winter skin care, staying active in winter, sun safety in summer
