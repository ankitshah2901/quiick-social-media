## 3 concrete changes
1. **Lead every reel with a practical how-to promise and a number** ("How to…", "4 checks…", "1 call to…"). How-to hooks
   (label, transfer) beat seasonal/product hooks this week on reach. Avoid vague seasonal hooks on weekends.
2. **Make Facebook drive clicks:** put the tracked link in the first 2 lines of the Facebook caption (currently near the
   end; 0 link clicks all week) and favour local/service topics on Facebook (transfer post was the best FB plan post).
3. **Ask for a reply people can answer in one word** in the first comment (e.g. "A or B?") and use a "Save this" /
   "Send this to…" cover cue on carousels. Zero saves, shares and real replies this week means the algorithm has no
   signal to push the posts further.
